About Us

Transforming HR through automation and innovation

AutoHR is a leading provider of automated human resources solutions designed to streamline HR processes, reduce administrative burden, and empower businesses to focus on what matters most: their people. Our comprehensive suite of tools combines cutting-edge technology with intuitive design to revolutionize how organizations manage their workforce.

Our Mission

At AutoHR, our mission is to transform human resources management through innovative technology that simplifies complexity, enhances efficiency, and fosters better workplace experiences for both employers and employees.

Our Vision

We envision a future where HR professionals are freed from administrative tasks to focus on strategic initiatives that drive organizational success and employee satisfaction. We aim to be the global leader in HR automation, setting the standard for excellence and innovation in the industry.

Company History

Since our founding in 2018, AutoHR has been committed to revolutionizing the HR landscape. What began as a small team of HR professionals and software engineers has grown into a global company serving thousands of businesses across various industries.

2018

AutoHR was founded in San Francisco by a team of HR professionals and software engineers with a vision to simplify human resources management through technology.

2019

Launched our first product - an automated employee onboarding solution - which quickly gained traction among small to medium-sized businesses.

2020

Expanded our product suite to include performance management, time tracking, and compliance modules in response to the changing work environment during the global pandemic.

2022

Secured Series B funding to accelerate growth and opened offices in New York, London, and Singapore to better serve our growing global client base.

2024

Rolled out our AI-powered HR analytics platform, enabling data-driven decision-making for organizations of all sizes, and surpassed 10,000 active customers worldwide.
